Xuemin Lin is a professor in the Department of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. From June 2022 to February 2026, he was a Chair Professor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and the founding Chair of the Department of Data and Business Intelligence at the Antai College of Economics and Management. He is a foreign member of Academia Europaea, a Fellow of IEEE, and a Fellow of AAIA. He received a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Queensland in 1992 and a BSc in Applied Mathematics from Fudan University in 1984.

Before joining Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Xuemin was a Scientia Professor at the University of New South Wales and the founding head of its Database and Knowledge Research Group. He began teaching at the University of Western Australia in 1994, following a research fellowship at the University of Queensland. By July 2026, 41 students had obtained PhDs under his supervision, according to his CV. He was Editor-in-Chief of IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering from January 2017 to December 2021, and served on the editorial boards of other major journals.

Xuemin's research interests lie in databases, data mining, AI, algorithms, and complexity. His current work concerns scalable computation, mining, and learning from large-scale data.

  • Research topics include graph data analysis, mining, and visualization; stream data analysis; spatial information systems; and uncertain data management.

  • He also works on real-time cloud computation, big data analytics, and web information systems. His Google Scholar profile showed 30,497 citations and an h-index of 87 on September 24, 2026.
